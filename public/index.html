Step 1 — the job

Who should hear
about your application?

  1. We search the web for hiring managers, recruiters, and people in this role.
  2. We find a real, publicly posted email at the company to learn its address format, then predict each person's email.
  3. AI ranks them and drafts a note from your template, personalized to each person.
  4. You review and edit. Nothing sends until you press Send.
  5. Each sent email is logged to your Google Sheet.
